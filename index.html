Who we are

A full horn section and nine reasons to dance.

The Soul Cats Band brings a nine-piece live show to the Central Coast — twin saxophones, a rhythm section that doesn't quit, and vocals out front that turn a dance floor on in the first eight bars.

We built our set list around the songs that make a room move: Motown, funk, classic soul, and the dance hits that bridge every decade from the '60s through right now. Weddings, wineries, breweries, private parties, festivals — we read the room and keep it moving.
